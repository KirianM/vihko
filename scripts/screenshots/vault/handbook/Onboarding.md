# Onboarding

Welcome aboard. Your first week, in order:

1. Open the sign-up link Hanna sent you and choose a password (10 characters or more).
2. Turn on two-step verification in Preferences › Account.
3. Read [[Welcome]] and [[How we write notes]].
4. Skim the last two weekly syncs in `meetings/`.
5. Write something in your personal folder, `people/<your name>`. Only you can see it.

Questions about a note go in a comment on that note, not in a chat.
